Component notes from the v3.2 design review. The palette, typography and spacing remain from the earlier version. Replaced components are listed at the end for reference.

Each card shows a photo, dish name, restaurant, neighborhood, menu price and distance. Images use a 4:5 ratio and names can wrap. Missing prices read "Price not listed". The same card is used in the feed, search and saves. Price is the listed menu price only. Distance is straight-line miles from Shaw, never a walk time. The card is one button whose accessible name reads the dish, restaurant, price, distance and any state chip. Save is a sibling control with aria-pressed, never nested. Without a photo the card is still complete.

State chip v3.2

Three states, one vocabulary
! Lists nuts? Ingredients unknownNo longer on menu
Marks a dish on a memory or context surface (saves, collections, restaurant menu) whose state needs attention: the menu lists an allergen the diner avoids; no ingredient information exists; the dish has left the menu. Recommendation surfaces never show the conflict chip, because conflicts are excluded upstream by the shared classifier. Top-left, kept clear of the save control. Use specific labels: "Lists nuts", "Ingredients unknown", "No longer on menu". Never "unsafe", never "safe". "!" for conflict, "?" for unknown, solid ink for unlisted: each state carries a glyph or treatment as well as a color. The chip text is part of the card's accessible name.

The active constraints, in the order they apply: lifestyle, allergies, price, distance. Each chip opens its own editor; browsing chips open the filter sheet, dietary chips open dietary settings. "Clear filters" clears browsing chips only. The same bar appears in search to show which filters are active. Allergy chips use the accent and the "!" glyph; nothing else on the bar does. Chips are buttons named "Browsing filter: within 1 mi. Edit" and so on. The bar is a labeled group.

The conflict note replaces the acknowledgement gate. It sits above the facts and the action. Below it the primary action changes: "See other dishes at [restaurant]" and a quiet "See the restaurant's menu"; there is no order control and nothing to unlock. The unknown note keeps the ordinary next step but precedes it. Three beats for a conflict: what the menu lists → that you asked to avoid it → where to go instead. Never "confirm to continue". role="note", not alert: the page opened with it. Border, glyph and text; never color-only.

Replaces the order sheet. The title and the one useful line depend on the available destination: the full menu, a dish on it, or a phone number. The sheet keeps the dish and restaurant visible and asks the diner to check current prices and availability on the menu. In the prototype the primary action ends at a neutral "End of prototype" state with Back; the disclosure that links are simulated sits in the caption beside the prototype, not inside it. A failure keeps the dish and offers a retry. Never promise a cart, a fee, an ETA or that the dish is in stock. No hostnames and no dates inside the app. A named role="dialog" that moves focus in, traps Tab, closes on Escape and returns focus to its opener. The endpoint is role="status".

Empty results, by cause v3.1 · v3.2

1 “steak” dish exists, but your settings exclude it
1 removed by your browsing filters (Under $15, within 0.5 mi).
Clear browsing filters
! 1 lists nuts, which you asked to avoid. Allergy settings are never cleared from here.
Three causes, three answers: nothing in the catalog; removed by a browsing filter; removed by a dietary setting. Each cause names its count and offers only its own recovery. Unknown-ingredient matches are listed below the note, not counted. Nothing is widened silently. "Exists, but your settings exclude it" is the headline whenever the catalog has the dish. Only lifestyle and browsing filters get a one-tap recovery. The allergy line carries the "!" glyph; recoveries are full-width buttons.

Lifestyle and allergy selection chips v2 · copy v3.2

Default / selected
Vegetarian✓ VeganNuts✓ Nuts
Lifestyle is single-select in ink; allergies are multi-select in the accent, because they are a different tier and must look like it. The step is optional at entry and always in Profile. Describe the behavior: "dishes whose menu lists one of these leave your feed and search; dishes with no ingredient information are listed separately and never counted as a match; saved dishes stay visible, labeled." Never "removed everywhere", never "safe". Real buttons with aria-pressed; selection is fill + check glyph, not color alone. Min target 44pt.

Menu status v3.2

Off the menu (saved dish)
No longer on the menuNo longer on menu
Replaces "Sold out today · usually back tomorrow" and "Closed now · opens 8 AM", both of which claimed an availability model the catalog lacks. The only status the data supports is whether a dish is still on the menu. Being listed never implies available tonight, and the copy says so. Pair the status with the consequence: "This dish is no longer listed on the menu. You can still keep it in your saves." Ink pill with text; the next-step sheet repeats the status so it is read at the point of commitment.

Loading skeleton · offline banner v3

You're offline. Showing your last loaded feed. Prices and availability may have changed since.
The skeleton mirrors the labeled card (photo block plus two text lines) so nothing jumps on arrival. The offline banner warns about staleness rather than hiding content. The location-off banner is gone: the area chip shows Shaw, DC, not a permission failure. Loading container is labeled; shimmer stops under prefers-reduced-motion. Banners are role="status".

Non-color indicators — summary v3 · v3.2

✓ selected! conflict? unknownoff menu
Every state carries a glyph or treatment alongside its color: selection = check, conflict = "!", unknown = "?" with a dashed or outlined treatment, off the menu = solid ink. This is the floor for any new component. Terracotta-on-cream passes 4.5:1 for text ≥13px; the unknown chip's outline passes 3:1 against the photo veil.

Retired in v3.2 what replaced them

Gone, and why
Trust & methodology disclosure → Facts list. No order records supported the score.
Order-sheet allergy acknowledgement → Dietary notes. Acknowledging a warning did not resolve the ingredient conflict.
Carrier rows with fees and ETAs; pickup timing → Next-step sheet. Invented operational data.
Search-result glass label → Labeled card. One card for every surface.
Availability status (closed · sold out) → Menu status. No hours model exists.
Allergen warn chip → State chip. Three states, not one.
Prototype-data label on scores → Source and date on every fact. Nothing evidence-shaped remains to label.
Each retirement came from the September 2026 design review, not from participant evidence. The earlier components were reasonable answers to the wrong question ("how should this number present?") when the right one was "can the prototype know this at all?".
