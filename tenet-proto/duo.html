WHAT’S REAL, WHAT’S SIMULATED
Dart, Meridian, quotes, fills, and the trading history are fictional. The rules, the interventions, the cross-surface sync, and the Record are fully functional — everything you do here lands in it.
WHAT TENET READS & STORES
Conceptually, Tenet runs inside the broker at order submit — an SDK the host embeds. It reads order tickets at send and fills after the close; never your screen, never other accounts. Your rules and Record are stored in your Tenet account.
SYNC & LEAVING
One rule set: stricter changes reach every host immediately; looser ones arm at the next open. Disconnect any time — rules stop arming, the Record stays yours, and deleting Tenet deletes both.
