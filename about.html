ABOUT

I design products and the brands they live in.

I'm Lana, a product designer with a background in visual communication. I care about what an interface asks people to understand, decide, and do next.

STORY

My current role is Graphic Design Specialist at IKEA. My background is in visual communication; my product work applies that attention to hierarchy, typography, and consistency to interactive decisions. This portfolio includes client UI and brand engagements alongside independent product concepts. Outside work, I paint.

APPROACH

I work through what happens before an action, what could go wrong, and how someone recovers. Tenet explores those questions through trading rules; Morsel through a visual restaurant menu. In client work, I adapt to an existing brief and visual system, as in Matrix, or develop a connected brand and interface, as in Hey Period.
